BUILT FOR YOUR COMPUTER

Settings

Compare engines using your own voice. Quality takes priority over speed.

On a 6 GB RTX 2060, run one model at a time and close other GPU-heavy applications. Actual VRAM fit and speed must be measured on your computer.

Private by default

No paid subscription or cloud voice API. Model downloads need internet; normal inference is offline. Profiles live in your Windows user’s local application-data folder with user-only access configured by the installer.

Use a separate Windows account for people who should not access recordings. This studio does not encrypt your disk or protect against software running as your own user.

Profile deletion removes its active recordings and evaluations. Copies in exports or backups must be removed separately; secure erasure on SSDs is not guaranteed.
