Model files are independently SHA-256 checked against pinned upstream metadata. Runtime and actual-neural reports and installer/source checksums accompany both releases.

The original specification allows honest unsupported-capability reporting for 13–17 and 36. Dedicated similarity adjustment, accent transfer and whisper synthesis are unavailable in the integrated Chatterbox APIs. Delivery presets are real processing approximations, and the local cosine comparison is not a similarity percentage or identity verification. Turbo accepts upstream laughter tags, but their acoustic delivery is not certified.

All hosted Windows neural checks used **CPU**. The CUDA installer bundles CUDA 12.4 PyTorch; physical RTX 2060 acceleration, 6 GB fit and speed remain unrun. No legitimate signing certificate has been established, so installers are unsigned. Clean consumer-PC installation, physical microphone behavior and personal family-voice fidelity remain separate acceptance checks. The user's reported successful recording/playback is consumer evidence, not certification of every device or all acoustic outcomes. These limits prevent calling all 46 requirements fully accepted.
